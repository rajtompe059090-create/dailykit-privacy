DailyKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

DailyKit ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the DailyKit mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

DailyKit is primarily an offline utility application and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features.

Information entered by you into tools such as Offline Notes is stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

2. Advertising

DailyKit uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information, including device identifiers and advertising-related information, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable laws.

For more information about how Google handles data for advertising, please review Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising ID

DailyKit may use the Android Advertising ID through Google AdMob for advertising and measurement purposes. Users may be able to reset or limit advertising personalization through their Android device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

DailyKit may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Offline Notes

Notes created using the Offline Notes feature are stored locally on your device. DailyKit does not intentionally upload your notes to a remote server.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

DailyKit is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Deletion

Since DailyKit's main features do not require a user account, there is no account information stored by us that requires account deletion. Locally stored app data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application from the device.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
