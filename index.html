DailyKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

DailyKit ("the app") provides everyday utility tools such as calculators, converters, notes and other productivity features.

Information We Collect

DailyKit does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the core features of the app.

Some information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the app, such as advertising services.

Advertising

DailyKit may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising information and app interaction data in accordance with Google's policies.

For more information, please review Google's advertising privacy information.

Google Privacy & Terms

Notes and Local Data

Information entered into the Notes feature is stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. DailyKit does not upload your notes to our own server.

Children's Privacy

DailyKit is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DailyKit, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
